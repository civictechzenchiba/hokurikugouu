August 2026 Ishikawa, Toyama and Fukui Heavy Rain Recovery Support Portal

This website is run by CTZC volunteers and is not an official government publication. Apply and make inquiries through the official Ishikawa, Toyama and Fukui or municipal contact points. Always check official websites for the latest information.

The Japan Meteorological Agency has not given this rainfall an official name. National and municipal documents call it “the heavy rain beginning 27 August 2026” (令和8年8月27日からの大雨), and in Fukui “the heavy rain beginning 29 August.” Use those Japanese wordings when searching official pages.

3Three things to do right now

  1. Take photos before you clean up All four sides, the water line, each room, and everything you throw away. You cannot take them again later.
  2. Keep every receipt Repairs, cleaning, lodging, and replacement purchases. You will need them for support programs and insurance.
  3. Check with your municipality before repairs Signing a repair contract first can make you ineligible for publicly funded emergency repairs.
